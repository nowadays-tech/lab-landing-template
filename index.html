Why build another notebook?

There is no shortage of good note-taking apps. I kept running into the same problem with all of them: they store a link, and a link is a promise someone else can break.

Half of the references in my old notes now point at a parked domain, a paywall, or a redirect to a homepage. The reading survived in my head. The source did not.

So Fieldnote saves the page itself. Text, images, stylesheets, the lot, written to a folder on your disk in formats you can open without this app. A capture from 2019 opens the same as one from this morning.

Everything runs locally. There is no account, no sync server, and nothing to cancel. If Fieldnote stops being maintained tomorrow, your library still opens in a browser.

  2. Step 2

    Fieldnote keeps the full document, not a stripped summary. Images, code blocks, and footnotes all render the way the author laid them out, with or without a connection.

  3. Step 3

    Shape it into a library worth keeping

    Group captures into collections, annotate them, and search the full text of everything you have ever saved. The good stuff stops disappearing into a bookmark folder.

Common questions

Can I try it before paying?
Yes. Thirty days with everything switched on, and no card up front. After that it is 39 EUR once, for the version you have and every update to it.
Do I need an account?
No. There is nothing to sign in to. Your licence is a file, your captures are a folder, and neither one needs a server to work.
Can it capture pages behind a login?
Yes. Fieldnote saves the page as your browser renders it, so anything you can read while signed in is captured the same way.
Is there a Windows version?
Not yet. The waitlist below is the honest answer, and the captures are open files, so a Windows build is not what stands between you and reading them.
